About

Engineering for the long run

I’m a backend engineer with a background in electrical and electronics engineering, and I enjoy the unglamorous parts that make systems dependable: clear domain boundaries, careful data modelling, idempotent operations and honest monitoring.

When a problem lands on my desk, I start with the failure modes. What happens under load, on retry, or when a dependency disappears? I prototype the riskiest part first, write the decision down, and then build in small, reviewable increments.

I’m moving toward distributed-systems design and platform engineering, and I like mentoring engineers through design reviews and pairing.

How I approach problems

  • Define the problem and constraints first
  • Model the data before the endpoints
  • Make it work, make it observable, then make it fast
  • Record decisions in short ADRs
  • Ship behind flags and measure
